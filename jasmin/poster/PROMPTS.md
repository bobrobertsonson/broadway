# Poster image prompts (splash-art style)

Style goal: crisp digital game splash art, not hand-drawn. Each prompt below is complete: the style
words are built in, and its negative prompt follows it. Generate on a 2:3 portrait canvas (prints at 24x36).

Generate pieces separately and composite them with the vector logo (`logo.svg`) in Figma. Ask for
no text in the art: generators mangle lettering. Attach real photos as face references, or the
people won't look like you.
